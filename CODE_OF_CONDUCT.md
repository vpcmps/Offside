# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Offside community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub Security Advisory for this repository. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
